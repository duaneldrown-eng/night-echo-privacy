Privacy Policy for Night Echo

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Night Echo is a dream journaling and interpretation application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Night Echo.

Information You Provide

Night Echo allows users to enter dream descriptions and related journal information. This information is used to provide the application's journaling and dream interpretation features.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

Night Echo may request access to your device's microphone when you choose to enter a dream using your voice. Microphone access is used only when you activate the voice-entry feature. Spoken input may be processed by speech recognition services to convert your speech into text.

AI Dream Interpretation

When you request a dream interpretation, the text you provide may be sent securely to an artificial intelligence service in order to generate the requested interpretation. Users should avoid entering information they do not want processed for this purpose.

How Information Is Used

Information entered into Night Echo is used to provide the app's features, including dream journaling, voice entry, and dream interpretation. Night Echo does not sell users' personal information.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by Night Echo. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Night Echo is not specifically directed toward children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Night Echo changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
